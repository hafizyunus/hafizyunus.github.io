## The team

NITKRacing is NITK's Formula Student team: students design, build and race a
single-seat race car. I was the team's Vehicle Dynamics Engineer and later Mechanical
Lead, heading the **Formula Bharat 2026** mechanical design and leading **25+**
mechanical engineering students across vehicle dynamics, suspension, brakes, vehicle
modelling, gearbox design and battery packaging.

## Lap-time simulation

To compare powertrain options, I wrote a **point-mass lap-time simulator in MATLAB**.
It estimates lap times for a track from a vehicle's properties, so design choices can
be compared by their effect on performance.

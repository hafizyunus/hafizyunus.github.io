# Project brief

> The "who / why / what" of the site. Design and content decisions should trace back here.
> Last updated: 2026-09-26

## Owner
Hafiz Yunus — **mechanical engineer**.

## Audience
1. **Recruiters / hiring managers**: need to scan experience quickly and get to the resume.
2. **Peers / engineering community**: interested in how the work was done (projects, methods, tools).

## Primary action
**Explore my work.** Projects/case studies are the centrepiece of the home page.
Secondary: resume (view/download) and contact.
GitHub is present but **not prominent** (a social link, not a hero CTA).

## Tone
Friendly professional: first person, warm, clear; clean design without gimmicks.

## Content decisions
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Photo | **No photo** (initials/monogram or simple mark instead) |
| Contact | **Email + LinkedIn** links, shown openly |
| Blog | **Later** (Phase 3) |
| Content source | Draft from resume placed in `_inbox/` (git-ignored), then reviewed |
| Visual style | Owner to share 2–3 reference sites |

## Implications for a mechanical-engineering portfolio
- Projects are **visual**: CAD renders, drawings, simulation (FEA/CFD) plots, test rigs, photos of built parts.
  → Project pages need strong image support: galleries, captions, before/after, lightbox.
- Show **engineering process**, not just results: requirements → concept → analysis → design → test → outcome, with numbers (loads, tolerances, weight/cost savings, efficiency).
- **Tools & methods** matter to readers: CAD (e.g. SolidWorks/CATIA/NX), FEA/CFD (ANSYS/Abaqus), GD&T, DFM, manufacturing processes, standards.
- Credentials (degree, EIT/PE or equivalent, certifications, patents, publications) deserve their own section.
- Confidential/NDA work: describe at a higher level, with generic or redacted visuals.
